The game plan

A control deck that ramps into colourless mana and spends it recasting the same handful of spells. It doesn't race. It survives until Tron is online, then grinds with blinks and recursion until a win condition sticks.

Piloting notes

From the PauperBrews guide, checked against this 75.
  • Mulligan aggressively for Tron. Keep three pieces, or two plus Expedition Map or Crop Rotation.
  • Against land destruction like Cleansing Wildfire, answer at instant speed with Crop Rotation: sacrifice the targeted land and fetch a replacement.
  • The base loop: Ghostly Flicker on a Wall and a Prophetic Prism returns the Flicker and draws a card. With two Walls, it returns the Flicker plus any other instant or sorcery.
  • Protect Murmuring Mystic from exile removal like Journey to Nowhere. Pulse of Murasa and Call Damage Control only rebuy creatures from the graveyard.
  • Breath Weapon deals 2 to each non-Dragon creature, your 1/1 Birds and Mulldrifter included. Mystic (1/5), the Walls (0/4) and Stonehorn (1/4) survive it, so cast it before you start making Birds.

Finding Tron

Twelve Urza lands, and the cards that find, protect or rebuy them. Every piece taps for one on its own. All three together make seven.

Colour from colourless

Tron makes only , and the deck casts spells in all five colours. These turn colourless mana into coloured mana.

What each splash is for

Blue is the base. The other colours come in through the filters above and a few dual-purpose lands.

Draw a sample hand

Shuffle the 60 and draw seven. The keep rule is the PauperBrews one: all three Tron pieces, or two plus Expedition Map or Crop Rotation. Take a mulligan and you draw seven new cards, then tap the ones to put on the bottom.

      This list vs PauperBrews

      The engine is the same: Tron, Walls, Ghostly Flicker, Mulldrifter and Mystic. Against the PauperBrews list, this one trades three Energy Refractors and the colour lands for cheaper fixing that does something else too, moves Stonehorn into the main, and adds Rolling Thunder as a finisher.
